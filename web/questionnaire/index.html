Engineering decision episode questionnaire

One ASIC physical-design decision, filled in by the engineer who made it, with no interviewer present. It turns a recollection into a schema-shaped episode record. Rejected paths are the most valuable material, so they are asked for explicitly.

Not publicly hosted. Whether to host it on AINumbers or Post Oak Labs has not been decided. This is a capture instrument for a research dataset; records are reviewed before any dataset inclusion.

This page makes no network calls, stores nothing (all state stays in this browser tab and is gone when it closes), keeps no analytics, does not ask for your name or employer, and collects no personally identifying information. The only output is a file you choose to download. Anonymous episodes are equally welcome.

1. Context

Coarse buckets are fine. The scale is descriptive context, not a metric.

2. Before: prior state

Before you spotted the problem, what was the state of the design? Give the milestone (post-CTS, post-route) and the number you were tracking. All metrics are optional but encouraged; leave blank any you do not recall.

A number that is a rough recollection is fine. A fabricated precision is not.

3. Observation

What did you actually see first? Which report, which line, which number? Keep this to what you saw, not what you concluded.

The failure class drives the cause pickers below.

4. Suspected cause

A picker plus free text. The picker is a vocabulary seed, not an answer key; tell the real story in the free text.

5. Candidates considered (the hard part)

List every intervention you considered, including the ones you rejected. A straight line with no rejected paths is almost never true. Draw a timeline: at the moment you saw the problem, what were your options and in what order did you eliminate them? Rejected paths are the most valuable material here. Exactly one candidate is marked as the one you took.

6. Chosen intervention

Walk through exactly what you changed, in the order you changed it. Do not let the tool supply the answer.

7. After: post state

What did the same reports say after?

8. Assessment

No name or employer is requested anywhere on this page.

9. Self review

Every prior metric improved at the same time. Real fixes usually cost something somewhere. If this one did not, say why in the rationale.

10. Output

The episode is self-reported and uninterviewed, and the record says so. Validation mirrors the edi-schema validator's cross-field rules.
